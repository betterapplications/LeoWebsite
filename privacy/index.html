Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Better Applications LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company, doing business as Leo (“Leo”, “we”, “us” and “our”), collects, uses and shares your personal information when you use the Leo app, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

We are committed to protecting your privacy, and we use your personal information only as described below and in line with applicable privacy laws.

1. What we collect

  • Your account. You sign in with Apple. We get a sign-in ID and your first name from Apple. You can change your name any time in Settings.
  • Your answers. What you tell us in setup, like your weekly goal, how often you work out, your workout style, gender and age.
  • Workouts. Your workout check-ins, photos taken during them and reactions on those photos. Photos are private between partners and never publicly accessible.
  • Partner information. When you pair with a partner using a code, we store who you're paired with and your connection status.
  • Goals and wagers. Your weekly goal, weekly results and streak, and the wager you and your partner pick.
  • Leo's stuff. Fish, badges and the items you buy or equip for Leo.
  • Notifications. A device token so we can tell you when your partner works out.
  • Subscription status. Whether you have an active subscription. Apple handles the payment, so we never see your card.
  • Device and usage info. Basic technical info such as your device type, app version and IP address, collected automatically by our service providers to run the app and process subscriptions.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Manage your Leo account
  • Connect you with your partner
  • Track weekly goals, streaks and history
  • Show your workout check-ins to you and your partner
  • Send reminders, notifications and partner updates
  • Manage your subscription
  • Provide customer support

We don't sell your data to anyone.

3. How we store and protect your information

  • Your data is stored securely with Supabase.
  • Access is permission-restricted, so only you and your partner can see your shared data.
  • Communication with our servers is encrypted using HTTPS.
  • Photos are stored securely and are never publicly accessible.

4. Photos and the camera

Leo uses the camera only when you snap a workout photo. Checking that a photo has a person in it happens on your phone. Your photos are never used in ads.

5. Who we share with

  • Your partner sees your workouts, photos, progress and wager.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (stores your data), Apple (sign-in, payments and notifications) and RevenueCat (subscriptions).
  • When the law requires it. We may share information if we believe it's needed to comply with the law, a court order or a legal request, or to protect the rights or safety of our users or others.
  • If the business changes hands. If Better Applications LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that deal.

We do not share data with advertisers or third-party marketers.

6. Your choices

  • You can see and update your information in the app.
  • You can turn off notifications any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • You can delete your account any time in Leo's Settings. That removes your account, your photos and everything you and your partner shared in Leo.
  • Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings.

For any other request about your data, email support@leofitness.app.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your data, except where we need to keep something to meet a legal obligation.

8. Kids

Leo is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact us

Questions? Email support@leofitness.app.
